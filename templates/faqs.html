Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about shopping, orders, payments, delivery and returns.

Keep your order ID after checkout. You can use the order details page to view the order information currently available in ShopKart. If you need additional assistance, visit our Contact page and include your order ID.

Available payment options are shown during checkout. ShopKart's current payment flow is a demonstration; it does not process real payments. Do not enter actual card details, UPI PINs or banking passwords.

Delivery timelines depend on the delivery arrangements for your order. ShopKart currently does not provide a verified delivery estimate, so please contact support for assistance with a specific order.

Order cancellation is not currently available through a dedicated self-service feature. Contact ShopKart support with your order ID to discuss your request.

Please contact ShopKart support with your order details before assuming a return or refund is available. The return and refund process has not yet been configured as an automated feature.

Visit our Contact page and submit your name, email, subject and message. Your message will be saved in ShopKart's contact database for review.

You can browse products without logging in. Creating an account lets you access the account features currently available in ShopKart.

Visit the Contact page, select Product Enquiry, and describe the product and your question.
