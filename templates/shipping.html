Shipping & Returns

Important information about delivery, cancellations, and returns at ShopKart.

1. Order Processing

After an order is submitted, review the order details and confirmation shown on the website. Processing times may vary. An order placed on this demonstration website does not automatically mean a real product will be packed or shipped.

2. Shipping and Delivery

Actual delivery availability, shipping charges, courier services, and delivery estimates must be confirmed before a real purchase. Any delivery information shown in the current project should be treated as illustrative unless a genuine shipping service is integrated.

  • Check your order details before checkout.
  • Provide accurate delivery information wherever requested.
  • Delivery dates cannot be guaranteed unless confirmed by the seller or courier.

3. Shipping Charges

The website may display delivery charges during checkout. Review the final order summary before submitting an order. Displayed charges are part of the project demonstration unless real fulfilment services and commercial terms have been established.

4. Order Cancellation

Cancellation options depend on the order status and the seller's actual policies. Contact the ShopKart team to ask about an order. Do not assume that an order has been cancelled unless you receive confirmation through an implemented order-management process.

5. Returns and Exchanges

For real purchases, eligibility for returns or exchanges should be confirmed before ordering, including any time limits, product condition requirements, and exclusions. This project does not promise a real return or exchange service unless one is implemented.

6. Refunds

Refund availability and timing depend on the seller's confirmed policy and the payment method used. Since the current website may use demonstration payment functionality, do not assume that a real payment or refund has occurred.

7. Damaged or Incorrect Items

For a real order, contact the seller promptly if an item arrives damaged, defective, or different from what was ordered. Include your order reference and relevant details, but do not send passwords or payment credentials.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about shipping, cancellations, returns, or refunds, contact the ShopKart team.
