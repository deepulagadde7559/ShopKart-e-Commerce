Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how ShopKart handles information.

1. Introduction

Welcome to ShopKart. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you use our website. By using ShopKart, you acknowledge this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, ShopKart may process information such as:

  • Account details, such as your name and email address.
  • Contact form details and messages you submit.
  • Order information needed to process and display your order.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate and troubleshoot the website.

Do not submit passwords, card numbers, banking credentials, or other sensitive information through the contact form.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage customer accounts.
  • Support shopping, checkout, and order-related functions.
  • Respond to customer questions and feedback.
  • Maintain website security and improve functionality.

4. Password and Account Security

ShopKart is designed to store account passwords using password hashing rather than storing them as plain text. Users should choose strong, unique passwords and keep their login details private.

5. Cookies and Browser Storage

The website may use session cookies to support login and other session-based features. Shopping cart information may also be stored in your browser's local storage. Clearing browser storage may remove locally saved cart information.

6. Payments

If payment screens are provided as a demonstration, they do not process real payments unless a genuine payment provider has been separately integrated. Do not enter real payment credentials into a demonstration payment form.

7. Sharing and Retention

Information should only be accessed or shared when necessary to operate the website, respond to requests, maintain security, or comply with applicable legal requirements. Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for its purpose.

8. Data Security

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect information. However, no website or method of electronic storage can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Choices

You can choose what information to submit through optional forms. You may also contact the ShopKart team to ask questions about information you have provided.

10. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when website features or information practices change. Any revised version should be published on this page with an updated effective date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us through the ShopKart Contact Us page.
